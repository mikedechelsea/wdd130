Our Rafting Trips

Choose Your Adventure

Whether you are looking for a relaxing introduction to white water rafting or an exciting river challenge, White Water Rafting Co. has an adventure for you. Our experienced guides are committed to providing safe, exciting, and unforgettable experiences for every guest.

Contact Us to Plan Your Adventure

Big Mallard Adventure

Experience the excitement of Big Mallard as you navigate beautiful river waters with our experienced rafting guides. This adventure is perfect for visitors who want a fun combination of scenery, teamwork, and exciting rapids.

Difficulty: Intermediate

Duration: Full Day

Half Day Adventure

Short on time? Our Half Day Adventure gives you the opportunity to experience white water rafting without spending the entire day on the river. Enjoy beautiful scenery, exciting rapids, and plenty of opportunities for fun.

Difficulty: Beginner

Duration: Half Day

Los Dientes del Diablo

For adventurers looking for a greater challenge, Los Dientes del Diablo provides an exciting river experience. Work together with your team while our professional guides help you safely navigate the rapids.

Difficulty: Advanced

Duration: Full Day
